From Blueprint to Grihapravesam Key Handover

Our proven 5-stage project roadmap guarantees structural safety, zero budget leakages, and committed delivery dates.

01

Soil Test & 3D Vastu Blueprint

Geotechnical soil bearing capacity analysis, traditional Vastu orientation, photorealistic 3D elevations, and DTCP/Municipality plan approvals.

Zero Approval Delays
02

Anti-Termite & Deep RCC Foundation

Chemical anti-termite soil treatment, Tata Tiscon Fe550D rebar cage, and laboratory-tested M20/M25 concrete plinth beam casting.

Seismic Safe
03

Brickwork & Monolithic Slab Casting

28-Day Curing

Pressure Tested
